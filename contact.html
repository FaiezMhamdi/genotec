Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What age groups are your courses suitable for?

My courses are designed for students aged 10 and above. Python programming starts at age 10+, robotics kits at 12+, and AI projects at 14+. Each course is tailored to the appropriate level.

Do I need prior programming experience?

No prior experience is needed! My beginner courses start from absolute basics. For robotics and AI courses, I recommend starting with Python programming first to build a solid foundation.

What equipment do I need for robotics kits?

The robotics kits include all necessary components. You'll need a computer with internet access for programming. Basic tools like screwdrivers are included, and I provide detailed setup guides.

Can I get personalized mentoring?

Yes! I offer one-on-one mentoring sessions for students who want personalized guidance. Contact me to discuss your specific needs and schedule availability.
