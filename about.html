About Us

Mectrion Group

Founded in 2026, Mectrion Group brings training, manufacturing, and consulting together under one roof — so ideas can be learned, built, and launched in a single ecosystem.

One Group, Three Divisions

Mectrion Group was created to close the gap between learning technology and using it in the real world. Each of our divisions covers a different stage of the journey — and they work best together.

Nexora

Technical Training

Hands-on training in digital fabrication, robotics, CNC, mechanical design, programming, and web development. From curious 8-year-olds to professionals and entire companies, Nexora builds real skills through project-based learning.

  • Robotics & embedded systems
  • 3D printing & digital fabrication
  • CNC & mechanical design
  • Programming & web development

Ages 8+ · students · professionals · companies

Makera

Part Manufacturing

Custom parts, functional prototypes, and on-demand manufacturing services. Makera turns designs into physical reality with precision fabrication — from a single prototype to small production runs.

  • Custom part manufacturing
  • Functional prototypes
  • On-demand production services

Makers · startups · engineering teams

Mectrion Consulting

Advisory & Strategy

Expert guidance for schools, training centers, and companies adopting modern technology. From setting up fabrication labs and robotics curricula to digital transformation strategy, Mectrion Consulting helps organizations plan, equip, and grow with confidence.

  • Lab & curriculum setup
  • Technology adoption strategy
  • Staff training & enablement

Schools · training centers · companies

How It Works Together

The three divisions reinforce each other, creating a complete cycle from learning to building to scaling.

1. Learn with Nexora

Students and professionals master fabrication, robotics, and programming through hands-on training.

2. Build with Makera

Skills turn into real products: custom parts and functional prototypes manufactured on demand.

3. Scale with Consulting

Organizations get expert advice to adopt technology, equip labs, and train their own teams.
